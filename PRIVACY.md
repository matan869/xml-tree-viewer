# Privacy Policy — XML Viewer & Formatter

Effective: 2026-08-28

**This extension does not collect, transmit, store, or share any data.**

- All XML parsing and rendering happens locally in your browser.
- No network requests are made by the extension — it works fully offline.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting, no accounts.
- The `storage` permission is used only to remember your theme preference
  (auto / light / dark) inside your own browser profile.
- Page access is required solely to detect and render XML documents; the
  extension does not read or modify any non-XML page.
